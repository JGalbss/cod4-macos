Bundled community maps
======================

This package may include pinned copies of these community-created Call of Duty 4 maps:

- MW2 Rust (mp_mw2_rust), by Stendby
  https://www.customapscod.com/map.php?id=1626
- Terminal (#mw2) (mp_mw2_term), by -]K6[-Grimm
  https://www.customapscod.com/map.php?id=1071
- SPS Scrapyard (#mw2) (mp_scrapyard), by PanzerMan & Dugynight
  https://www.customapscod.com/map.php?id=1070

These maps are third-party community content. They are not part of this project's
GPLv3 source code and are not Call of Duty 4 retail game data. Their names and
credits are reproduced for attribution. The packager verifies every included map
file against mac/community-maps.sha256 before it creates the application bundle.
